## Evidence level and counterexamples not yet ruled out

**What is established:** a deliberately engineered, bounded two-timescale
model can change a local predictive distribution after consistent physical
counterevidence while preserving lifetime observations and restart/replay
identity. The mechanism outperforms the fixed whole-history predictor on
the predeclared late changed contexts in both source directions.

**What is not established:** the rules for detecting a change were authored;
the world choices, changed-state locations and complete probing schedules
were researcher-specified for measurement. This is *not* evidence that
original Ora autonomously chose any of those probes, learned new spatial
representations or developed new organism-like abilities. Six of six conditions
passed against **only the fixed engineered world and source variants**.
It does not settle sensor noise robustness beyond one sparse injected anomaly,
open-ended adaptation, utility of actions, memory costs under hard resource
budgets, evolutionary reproduction, conscience or consciousness.
