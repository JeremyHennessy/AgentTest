## Research inspiration, not evidence about Ora

- Oudeyer, Kaplan and Hafner (2007), sections III.B–C:
  https://www.pyoudeyer.com/ims.pdf . Motivates matching contexts before calling
  changes in prediction “progress.” This protocol uses exact context matching
  and a common-outcome proper score; it does not claim to implement that paper.
- Burda et al., Large-Scale Study of Curiosity-Driven Learning:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.04355 . Motivates distinguishing model disagreement
  from permanently high unpredictable error. The unresolved model is a testable
  design hypothesis, not a guarantee against noise fixation.
- Forestier et al. (2022):
  https://jmlr.org/papers/volume23/21-0808/21-0808.pdf . Motivates treating a
  finite goal/representation space as a limit on what can be discovered, rather
  than presenting searches inside it as unbounded invention.
