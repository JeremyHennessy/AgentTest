# Phase 42: inquiry-owned action, first slice

Status: design for independent review, 2026-10-07. No implementation, live activation, experiment, or learning claim is authorized by this document itself.

## Decision and scope

Build a default-off, copied-only investigation executive beside the existing cycle. Its first deliverable is a restart-safe ownership transaction:

public observation/context → durable inquiry and competing explanations → actual ordinary selection → frozen one-step predictions and one-use action → saved actual outcome → versioned interpretation.

Separate three gates:

1. **Transaction correctness:** this slice. Exact owner, at most one current capability, durable outcome, no replay, no hidden actuation.
2. **Inquiry/policy invention:** subsequent work. Better question formation, context-sensitive action choice, attention, and resumption. The temporary legacy selector is replaceable, not the proposed final architecture.
3. **Scientific evidence:** subsequent controlled studies of prediction, behavior, transfer, and retention. Neither a completed transaction nor a new belief-version number passes this gate.

Old Phase 42 quotas and roadmap targets do not constrain the design. Preserve their historical counters, evidence ledgers, and raw results as history; retiring a target does not erase or relabel old evidence. Do not tune ranking, promote a candidate to meet a target, force resumption, or count harness-scheduled actions as ordinary selection. A natural run that selects no executable inquiry is a valid null result.

## Minimal code surface proposed for implementation

Add a small research package under `experiments/inquiry_executive/`, with no import from production `core.py`, CLI, heartbeat, or workflows:

- `contracts.py`: strict bounded schemas, canonical hashes, identity and cross-reference invariants.
- `store.py`: one authoritative copied capsule, locking, atomic commits, recovery validation.
- `bridge.py`: temporary ordinary-agenda bridge and the narrow copied Challenge adapter. Public read interface and private transition interface remain distinct.
- `executive.py`: admission, selection, precommit, consumption, interpretation, and recovery.
- `tests/test_inquiry_executive_*.py` at repository test level: contract, ownership, crash, and preservation checks.

Four explicit interfaces, all disabled unless `enabled=True` on a validated copied capsule:

- `admit(inquiry_proposal, expected_revision)` creates/extends a grounded inquiry; grants no action authority.
- `select_next(public_inputs, expected_revision)` runs ordinary selection and returns a durable decision plus zero or one precommit.
- `execute(attempt_id, expected_revision)` reloads/revalidates the precommit, applies one pure copied transition, and durably saves its complete result.
- `recover_or_interpret()` validates the capsule and finishes already-committed interpretation; never invents a world result or replays a committed action.

`read()` has no side effects. No arbitrary command, selected-question override, forced-winner flag, score override, or execution-by-experiment-ID API.

## Copied capsule and trust boundary

One file owns copied Ora selection state, copied world, recorder/public frames, inquiries, decisions, attempts, outcomes, belief versions, monotonic counters, bounded budget, and transition events. Store its source-code manifest and immutable run identity. The manifest covers the reachable Core/agenda/admission/validation dependencies, new executive code, sensor/command/transition code, and interpreter, not just PR217’s seven experiment files. Do not split authoritative capability, outcome, or belief state into independent files. Optional human-readable exports are derived and disposable.

### Explicit small-proof limits

Full pre/post Core snapshots deliberately favor auditability over storage efficiency. This capsule is a small bounded transaction proof, not the sustainable long-trial persistence design. Sustainable history, compaction without lost evidence, and long-horizon resource cost remain a separate unsatisfied gate.

Choose one immutable profile at creation (MiB = 1,048,576 bytes):

- `synthetic_contract`: defaults **2 MiB total canonical capsule bytes, 8 committed decisions/cycles, 4 consumed actions**. Explicit lower/custom limits are allowed; hard maxima are **16 MiB, 16 decisions/cycles, 8 actions**. Actions cannot exceed the decision limit. No automatic increases.
- `preserved_input_smoke`: separately declare exactly one already-preserved input and its immutable hash, one invocation, **1 committed decision/cycle and at most 1 consumed action**, with an explicit byte cap no greater than **256 MiB**. There is no implicit larger-byte default. Select a sufficient cap from measured input size before running; existing retained inputs are approximately 48 MiB and full snapshot duplication matters. This exception does not permit prefix reconstruction, seed sweeps, historical study reruns, repeated smoke horizons, or mixing synthetic results into natural evidence.

Every committed selection attempt counts against the decision/cycle limit, including no-agenda-decision and no-action nulls. Completion/recovery of an existing action does not consume another decision or action allowance. Admissions still obey the total byte limit. Once a limit is reached, refuse new work without changing the capsule; T2/T3 recovery for existing valid work remains permitted within reserved capacity. Changing a profile/limit on an existing run is forbidden. Any larger-study or storage proposal requires separately reviewed scope; it cannot extend this proof by restarting with reset counters or reusing the smoke input after observing its result.

Before T1, serialize the entire candidate capsule in memory, including all copied snapshots, IDs, events, checksum, and newline. Reject projected oversize or excess decision/action capacity with every authoritative byte and counter unchanged. An action-bearing T1 must additionally reserve **1 MiB** for its complete T2/T3 outcome and interpretation. New public frames, receipts, and belief records each have a **64 KiB** canonical limit; the validated fixed Challenge schema and all other added/reserialized history, recorder, attempt, metadata, and event fields must have enforced bounds yielding a tested conservative maximum for complete T2 plus T3 growth within that reserve. The 1 MiB reservation is valid only with that schema-derived proof; it is not a guessed allowance. Do not preview the future transition to estimate size or feed future observations into selection. If a provable output bound is unavailable, grant no capability. Persist the outstanding completion reservation on T1. Every intervening commit, including admission and null selection, must fit its full serialized bytes plus the schema-derived remaining completion bound. After T2 retain the proven T3 remainder; release it only with canonical T3, or release the full reservation when an unconsumed prepared attempt is cancelled. No other work may borrow that capacity and strand an action. Recheck exact size before every atomic commit; never truncate lineage to fit. Boundary tests must verify that the reserve suffices and that an oversized result leaves the pre-transition copied world unchanged.

The executive alone can access the private copied world. Inquiry construction, selection, command proposals, predictions, and belief interpretation receive only immutable public views. The first adapter uses the already existing Challenge sensor, command grammar, and pure transition; it does not introduce a new world or external side-effect target. No network, shell, repository writer, arbitrary callback, or live-state capability is exposed to policies. Exactly-once durable effects are claimed only for this deterministic simulator, whose world, consumption, and full outcome share one atomic store. External effects would require a separate receipt/idempotency/reconciliation contract and are out of scope; do not build a generic recovery stack.

Creation requires explicit copied source inputs, a new output path under a designated research directory, and a manifest recording original source hashes. Reject live/default state locations, aliases of protected inputs, symlinks, hardlinks, missing paths on reopen, mismatched run/path identity, and unsupported schemas. Pin canonical capsule location and directory identity at creation; validate again under the lock and use no-follow file operations. Existing production stores remain read-only inputs. Copy manifests and SHA256 protect consistency, not authenticity against a malicious writer. Deliberate rollback of the entire capsule at the same location is outside this local trusted-storage threat model; no external-world exactly-once guarantee is claimed.

The adapter descriptor fixes source, world instance, actor, public sensor version, and action grammar. Admission freezes the exact validated source publication, grounding records, selected feature/relation contract, and public feature namespace. On subsequent attempts revalidate this original grounding against the pinned source and check that the same declared feature is currently publicly available. Unknown source/feature, wrong actor/world, or cross-source substitution rejects; temporary feature absence defers. Stable identity removes only the requirement to equal a newly generated current-publication candidate, not these source checks. Public frames carry source identity, monotonic sequence, predecessor hash, observation ID, exact public payload hash, and source receipt when present. Context is an explicit projection of observed public fields with its projection version and missingness; no latent world tags, future observations, or private cause labels. Preserve the full immutable public evidence/receipt/prediction lineage for both unresolved and completed outcomes and every retained belief version. At a storage bound, stop with `capacity_exhausted`; do not prune that lineage or reset counters.

## Stable identity and immutable records

IDs are allocated from durable counters scoped by a unique run ID. Never derive durable inquiry identity from text, array length, current publication hash, current winner, or a bounded agenda index.

- **Inquiry:** `inquiry_id`, originating public references, question, immutable hypothesis-set versions, current belief version, lifecycle status, and predecessor/successor links when the question's meaning changes. Each version has 2–8 explicitly competing explanations and their scope/limits. Changing wording alone does not create evidence; changing meaning creates a linked inquiry, not an in-place rewrite.
- **Legacy binding:** explicit one-to-one mapping from `inquiry_id` to a unique existing copied question ID and the relevant question/experiment contract hashes. Text deduplication or duplicate IDs causing ambiguity produce `binding_conflict`, never silent rebinding. Admission may stage a validated passive native/public contract through existing read-only-to-world APIs; this neither selects it nor grants authority.
- **Decision:** executive decision ID, copied cycle, pre/post selection-state hashes, exact ordinary agenda decision and policy/code manifest, candidate count and retained candidate summaries (explicitly truncated when applicable), complete pre-cycle inputs, actually selected question/thread, mapped inquiry or null, and explicit deferral reason. Persist independently of legacy history trimming.
- **Attempt/precommit:** fresh attempt ID, owning inquiry and immutable hypothesis/belief versions, exact decision ID, command and command-policy version, public context/frame hashes, prediction contract, source/world/actor identity, budget ordinal, capsule path/run identity, expected capsule revision, and status. An inquiry may have many historical attempts; globally only one unconsumed attempt is permitted.
- **Outcome:** unique outcome ID, attempt/decision/inquiry IDs, complete actual command receipt, before/after public frames, copied world revision hashes, and observation/evaluation status. Blocked movement is still a consumed action with a real outcome.
- **Belief version:** predecessor version, exact outcome and prediction hashes, interpretation-rule version, per-hypothesis case-local evaluation, unresolved alternatives, and explicit change/no-change reason. Permit exactly one canonical interpretation per `(attempt_id, outcome_id)`; the rule version is provenance, not another opportunity for credit. Reject changed-rule reinterpretation or migration unless a later design separately reviews it.

Predictions are frozen before actuation. The first slice permits a small declarative language over selected public feature values: equal, different, bounded categorical outcome, or explicitly unavailable; no executable expressions. Each hypothesis states its predicted observable outcome under this command and context, plus what would contradict it. Evaluation can be supports-this-case, contradicts-this-case, or unevaluable. Agreeing alternatives remain undiscriminated. These are case-local belief revisions, not global truth or calibrated probabilities. No retrospective editing, post-outcome prediction generation, or implicit conversion of missingness into refutation.

## Ordinary selection bridge, explicitly temporary

To avoid modifying the legacy path, the bridge uses a deep-copy in-memory implementation of Core’s `load/save/append_journal` store contract with a validated non-live path. `load` returns the same pre-cycle committed snapshot until Core saves, including repeated reads. Its staged state and complete cycle event have no independent persistence; they enter the capsule only after validation.

The first-slice envelope is deliberately narrow: `observation=None` (no repository sensor input), cognition/provider calls off, both lab flags false, all copied-public file/early-admission/dispatch/frontier flags off, `_phase42_counterfactual=False`, and `_withhold_current_prediction_evidence=False`. Public source observations and passive native contracts enter the capsule/in-memory copied state through grounded admission before the cycle. They are not passed as repository observations. The ordinary cycle retains its generation/ranking/selection path, and its existing no-current-prediction branch corrects provisional resumption accounting without a recursive scratch replay. Record `repository_observation=omitted_by_first_slice` and `legacy_resumption_diagnostic=no_current_prediction_evidence`, test these facts, and reject caller attempts to expand this envelope. A later repository-observation integration needs separate review.

The bridge neither filters competitors to make an inquiry win nor relabels the generated legacy question as the investigation. The caller cannot supply `legacy_question`, selection output, or an override selector. Persist the complete pre-cycle snapshot and inputs, internally produced cycle result/event, and post-cycle selection state. The ordinary decision reports total candidate count but retains only a bounded candidate summary: disclose that limitation. Complete preselection candidate instrumentation is not needed for this ownership slice and is deferred. Do not patch global selector functions, run a second selection, or duplicate ranking to manufacture that manifest.

Require a unique actual latest agenda decision for that cycle, selected question equal to the ordinary returned question, matching foreground thread, a valid unique inquiry binding, and an exact owned returned experiment consistent with `experiment_routing`. An intention-target/follow-up experiment belonging to another question grants no authority. If the winner is unbound, unsupported, unavailable, or no decision exists, record selection plus `no_executable_owned_inquiry` and do nothing. Do not execute a different “ready” inquiry. A selected inquiry without a valid bounded prediction or public command also defers without consuming budget.

For the first slice the existing public command selector may be retained as a named **compatibility control** using only public observation and recorded associations. Its known context limitation is declared. Its version/hash is recorded on every attempt, the interface admits later replacement, and no code here repairs its scores or claims it learns. Freeze predictions against the actual proposed command. Correctly binding that command to its owner does not establish that it discriminates the competing hypotheses; this needs separate evidence. PR217 can remain as an unchanged historical control; new stable inquiry validation must not require reconstructing a current-publication candidate ID after every action.

Every copied decision reports `planning_lab_actuation=deferred_by_inquiry_executive`, original requested mode, and `action_lab_actuation=disabled`. This is an intentional experimental treatment: copied executive cycles differ from legacy planning-enabled cycles. Do not present it as baseline equivalence. Normal `AgentCore.cycle`, flag defaults, production schema, and all enabled legacy lab behavior remain exact.

## State machine and durable boundary

All mutations use one exclusive process lock, reload, strict schema/cross-reference validation, and expected-revision compare-and-swap. Each commit writes a unique temporary file, flushes/fsyncs it, atomically replaces the capsule, then fsyncs its directory. Unsupported filesystem durability fails the acceptance gate. Journal events live inside that same revision; a separate journal is not required for recovery. Checksums alone do not serialize writers.

1. **Admit:** retain grounded inquiry/history. A new observation can justify a new inquiry/hypothesis version but cannot rewrite older predictions.
2. **Select and precommit (T1):** run the bridge without world actuation; commit ordinary decision, selected owner, frozen prediction, exact public command, fresh one-use attempt, and copied Ora post-selection state together. No valid action gives a decision with a null attempt. Precommit status is `prepared`.
3. **Consume (T2):** under lock reload exact T1 revision and all bound identities/hashes; check still-selected owner, unchanged public frame/source/code, command availability, pending count, and budget. Compute the pure copied transition locally. Commit changed world, consumed budget, `committed` attempt, full receipt and public observation atomically. Return only after commit. This is the world/outcome restart boundary.
4. **Interpret (T3):** deterministically interpret the saved outcome against T1 predictions, append one belief version, mark attempt `interpreted`, and retain lineage. Interpretation need not change hypothesis standing. It cannot call the world. A failed/unknown interpretation leaves `committed`, exposes its blocker, and blocks new actuation until resolved or explicitly quarantined by a future reviewed mechanism.
5. **Continue:** a later ordinary decision can choose this inquiry again or another. A new attempt requires a new decision, fresh public frame and pre-action predictions. An old hypothesis can persist across frames; old action authority cannot.

Inquiry lifecycle (`open`, `suspended`, `closed`) is separate from attempt lifecycle (`prepared`, `cancelled`, `committed`, `interpreted`). Selecting another inquiry records suspension plus new decision lineage; it does not imply evidence-caused resumption. New selection or accepted new input cancels any unconsumed attempt in the same commit before issuing another. A cancelled/stale capability cannot become valid again when an old inquiry later wins. Cancellation does not consume world budget.

Crash before T1: no authority exists. Crash after T1: unchanged prepared authority may execute once after fresh validation. Crash during pure transition before T2: no persisted world effect occurred; retry may recompute it. Crash after T2, including before a response reaches the caller: recovery returns the stored outcome or interprets it; never transitions again. Crash during T3: atomically either old belief or the complete new version. Restart alone never promotes, chooses, or resumes an inquiry.

A mutation of inquiry, decision, frame, source code, command, budget, world, path, or run identity invalidates a prepared attempt. Reject it before any transition. Same-ID/mutated-body requests, cross-inquiry outcome substitution, duplicate IDs, skipped sequence links, wrong-source evidence, and stale expected revisions are conflicts, not repair opportunities. After full store/run/request identity validation, check for an already committed identical attempt before applying the mutating revision precondition: its retry may return the saved result idempotently even though T2 advanced the capsule revision. It must never consume again. An uncommitted attempt requires the exact current T1 revision. Conflicting/invalid requests fail closed with all authoritative capsule bytes, counters, and lineage unchanged. Valid null selections may commit a truthful decision; this is distinct from accepting an invalid authority request.

## Deferred science and activation

Unmet gates remain explicit: selecting authored goals versus generating goals in an authored representation versus revising that representation, endogenous useful inquiries, genuinely discriminating hypotheses, context-sensitive command choice, outcome-informed subsequent policy, warranted suspension/resumption, and retained improvement on held-out tasks. Generalized same-prior-state/full-cycle causal measurement is later work, with public evidence withheld at its real intake boundary and all downstream effects recomputed. This slice must not reuse the narrow repository-prediction counterfactual as proof for the new source.

Synthetic controls prove plumbing only. A natural zero-selection case stays zero; memory visibility is not memory use; repeated or blocked actions are not learning; case-local interpretation is not transferable knowledge. Deployment/activation and any new world remain separate decisions outside this design slice.

Research inspiration, not validation: [Oudeyer et al. 2007](https://www.pyoudeyer.com/ims.pdf), III.B–C, motivates testing prediction progress within matched contexts; [Burda et al., Large-Scale Study of Curiosity-Driven Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.04355) motivates testing noisy-distractor/surprise traps. Later protocols must test these hypotheses in Ora. Neither paper nor this transaction design establishes a discovery capability.
