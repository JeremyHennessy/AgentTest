# Frozen copied-state evidence study: negative readiness result

Frozen execution and reports: October 7, 2026. Research only. No live world,
policy, score, agenda priority or provider changed. This is not activation approval.

## Result

- Retained associations changed the research selector's command in **32/32**
  matched comparisons. This establishes influence, not benefit.
- Retaining a new native outcome and its normal resolution changed the next
  ordinary question/intention in **0/8 default-flag pairs** and **0/8 production-
  flag pairs**. The latter enable strict admission and Planning Lab.
- There were five matched prior-disconfirmation opportunities. The retained
  selector repeated the same expectation in **5/5**; ablated choices repeated
  **0/5**, but those alternate commands were not executed and have no measured
  success. All five failures were seed 2, actions 4–8: west after an already
  blocked west attempt in the same public context at [2, 2].
- Only **4/32** harness actions had the study inquiry selected as ordinary
  foreground. The other 28 actions were still scheduled by the copied research
  harness through its bounded capability gate. They were not autonomous native
  foreground-selected actions.
- Eight raw foreground returns went to older questions Q000022/Q000023. None had
  new-evidence priority support; genuine resumption counts stayed unchanged at
  zero, and no return was to the staged inquiry. There were 28 original study-
  question relevance opportunities and four production-probe opportunities,
  with zero resumed selections in each configuration.

**Readiness is not established.** Do not present the selector contrast as Ora
learning better, the alternate commands as successful avoidance, or raw returns
as eight evidence-caused resumptions. No Phase 42/43 milestone follows.

## Read-path interpretation

Retained native outcome evidence reaches agenda telemetry: the study question
has priority 0.50 versus 0.35 with the new outcome withheld, reflecting the new
experiment, outcome and reflection references. Higher-priority ordinary questions
still win. Do not call this lost evidence or justify agenda-score tuning.

The recorder also incorporates the blocked transitions. The frozen selector
consumes global feature/action association counts, without conditioning on
location or the local blocked receipt. Its expectation declines but continues
to win. This identifies a representation/policy limitation, not a stale cache.
Any context-aware candidate must be a separate predeclared research experiment,
with disjoint held-out contexts and no route hints, reward target or special
ban on west. Changed context must permit a legitimate retest.
