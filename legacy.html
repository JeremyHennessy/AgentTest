Research frontier

The long-term goal, the implemented foundation, and what the evidence supports.
current-world · null result

The goal is autonomous discovery and growth: Ora should form useful questions from experience, choose bounded investigations, remember what happened, revise beliefs, and use that knowledge to guide later action. Progress means increasingly adaptive behavior backed by evidence, not just more activity or higher counters.

Running now

The existing bounded lab

The Bounded Action / Planning Lab, persistent memory and legacy inquiry agenda remain the live world. A bounded rollout reached the real heartbeat and durable storage at cycle 6516. This observer reads the growth branch; its phase and counters do not certify the redesign.

Merged · heartbeat integrated

An investigation owns its action

The implemented foundation links a selected investigation to its pre-action predictions, one bounded action, the actual outcome and a versioned belief revision. Ownership, evidence and receipts persist across restart; stale or reused authority is rejected.

Implemented · finite scope

Movement guided by evidence

The new policy derives movement hypotheses from public observation features, selects an eligible test, and uses retained evidence to condition later predictions. Discovery is frozen to the first 32 genuine observation rows; its context-conditioned hypotheses, authored features and model choices are finite. Unrestricted question generation and representation growth remain future work.

Phase 42 redesign: the current-world heartbeat adapter and durable receipt path have been exercised. The first enabled tick prepared a null case and took no owned action. Learning benefit and new-world readiness are not established. Existing agenda and resumption counters remain legacy diagnostics, not redesign acceptance gates. This foundation is not a complete open-ended Phase 42, and is not evidence of life or consciousness.
