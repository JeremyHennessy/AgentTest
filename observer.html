WHAT WOULD JUSTIFY ACTIVATION?

Three independent gates.

  1. Prediction benefit. A registered narrow positive result exists in copied worlds.
  2. Commitment continuity. Matched inherited goals and history must survive learner-owned control.
  3. Persistent execution. Verify durable state, one controller, bounded budgets and a rollback path.

Neither a phase number nor a passing software test can substitute for these checks.
